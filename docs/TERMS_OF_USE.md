# Terms of Use — SendWiseCampus (Student-Facing)

*Plain-English terms. If anything below conflicts with your rights under Indian law or your college's student charter, the stronger protection wins.*

## 1. What SendWiseCampus is

SendWiseCampus is a browser extension (and, on some tablets, a keyboard) installed on **college-owned devices** and connected to **campus WiFi**. It watches text you type into messaging and social sites for signs of cyberbullying — targeted at *you* or written *by* you — and shows a gentle warning before you send.

## 2. What it does

- Runs a cyberbullying detector **on the device**. Your text is analysed locally.
- If a warning fires, shows an overlay: **Edit / Send anyway / Cancel**.
- Sends **metadata only** to the college wellbeing team (see Privacy Notice).
- Nudges you to counselling contacts after repeat warnings.

## 3. What it does NOT do

- **No keystroke logging.** The detector runs on committed text segments; individual keystrokes are not recorded.
- **No content upload.** The words you typed never leave the device. Not to the college, not to a cloud service, not to us.
- **No screen recording.** Ever.
- **No browsing history collection.** Only the *host* (e.g. `instagram.com`) of a fired event is recorded, not URLs or page contents.
- **No use in academic misconduct or disciplinary proceedings.** Data from this platform is inadmissible in those forums by policy.
- **No sale, no research, no sharing** of individual data. Aggregate metrics may be published only with ethics-board approval.

## 4. Opt-out

- On a **college-owned device**: opt-out means returning the device. The extension is a condition of use on college hardware, the same way antivirus is.
- On **campus WiFi**: opt-out means using mobile data.
- On a **personal device**: install is voluntary; uninstall at any time from the browser's extensions page.

## 5. How the wellbeing team sees your data

- **Aggregate first.** The default dashboard shows counts per course, per hostel, per time window. Never your name.
- **Dual-control drill-down.** Any per-student view requires the wellbeing lead **and** the student ombudsman to both approve, both signed into the audit log, and only when a documented incident exists (you asked for help, someone reported you, or you reported someone).
- The wellbeing team **cannot** discipline you, mark down your grades, expel you, or refer you to police using this data. They can only offer support.

## 6. Retention

- Aggregate metadata: **1 academic year**, then automatically deleted.
- Per-student data (only if drill-down was authorised): **6 months** from incident closure, then automatically deleted.
- Deletion is enforced by a scheduled job, not a promise.

## 7. Your rights

- **Right to review.** You can request a copy of any metadata linked to your anonymous user hash. The ombudsman helps you do this.
- **Right to complain.** If you think the platform is being misused, contact the student ombudsman (see below). Complaints are logged and reviewed quarterly.
- **Right to be forgotten.** If you leave the college, all your records — aggregate or otherwise — are purged within 30 days.
